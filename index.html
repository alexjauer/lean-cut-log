Maintenance is about 2,700–2,800 on training days with a desk job, so 2,200 is a ~500 calorie deficit, about 1 lb of fat a week.

Rules that matter most

  • Protein every meal: 35–45 g at each of 4 meals.
  • Track the first 4 weeks in an app; most people underestimate by 20–30%.
  • Water: about 100 oz (3 liters) a day, 120 oz on gym days. Track it on the Water tab.
  • Alcohol: 2 drinks a week at most.
  • Fiber: 30+ g a day from vegetables, fruit, oats, beans.
  • Sleep 7–8 hours. Short sleep raises hunger and costs muscle.
  • Holidays: Thanksgiving (Nov 26) and Christmas are one-day maintenance meals, not a week.
  • Supplements: creatine 5 g daily, plant-based protein powder (pea or soy), vitamin D in winter if low.

Grocery list · one week

  • Protein: 3 lb chicken breast, 2 lb salmon, cod or shrimp, 2 lb 93% lean ground turkey or beef, 18 eggs, 2 cartons egg whites, 2 tubs nonfat Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, 2 blocks extra-firm tofu or tempeh, canned tuna, plant-based protein powder
  • Carbs: oats, whole-grain bread, rice or quinoa, potatoes and sweet potatoes, whole-wheat tortillas, canned lentils or black beans, bananas, apples, frozen berries
  • Fats: olive oil, avocados, almonds, peanut butter, hummus
  • Vegetables: broccoli, spinach, peppers, onions, green beans, bagged salad, frozen stir-fry mix
  • Extras: salsa, hot sauce, mustard, soy sauce, spices
